title: "Research Theme: Exposure Fairness and Transparency in Information Access"
permalink: /theme-fairness/
author_profile: true
---

Information access systems mediate what information gets exposure.
This raises several concerns of fairness, diversity, transparency, and privacy in the context of information access.
This research theme focuses on the study and mitigation of allocative and representational harms from disparate exposure.
